# Victory conditions: ideas for discussion

**Status: proposal.** Nothing here is built except what's marked *(today)*. The goal is that each empire
wins in ways that fit its background and ideology, and is barred from some that don't. Pick, cut and
tune, then we build.

## How it would work
- Each victory has a **track** with visible progress in a Victory screen, so players can see who is close.
- A game turns victories on or off as today; each faction can only win through the victories its
  ideology allows (table below). A faction that can't win a way can still *stop* others from winning it.
- The game also ends when **no great power survives** (no winner), when **every human is eliminated** (the
  leading computer power wins), or at the **turn limit** (highest score) *(today)*.

## Candidate victories

### Conquest
| Victory | Condition | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Conquest** *(today)* | Hold every other great power's home system. | The classic. |
| **Domination** *(today)* | Own 40% of all star systems at the end of a round. | Territory-based; less bloody than conquest. |
| **Hegemony** | Hold 2 other great powers' homes **and** have 4 minor factions as vassals. | Rule through fear and subjects. |
| **Glorious Campaign** | Win 60 battles (a battle where you lose fewer ships than the enemy), including at least one against every other great power. | Warrior-culture victory: it's about the fighting, not the land. |

### Economic
| Victory | Condition | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Trade Empire** | Run active trade routes with every surviving great power and 4 minors for 10 consecutive turns. | Needs the trade routes now being built. |
| **Economic Supremacy** | Produce 35% of the galaxy's total income (energy + metals + exotics) for 10 consecutive turns. | Pure economy, no diplomacy needed. |
| **Planned Economy** | Complete five Five-Year Plans and hold the largest population in the galaxy. | A Krasnyr-flavoured economic win. |

### Technological
| Victory | Condition | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Ascendance** *(today)* | Research every tech in 4 fields and build the Ascension Gate. | |
| **Singularity** | Research every tech and keep 3 Research Labs running at Core Worlds for 10 turns. | The Lattice's natural victory. |
| **Ancient Secrets** | Survey 8 ancient ruins and research Ascension Theory. | Exploration-driven science. |

### Diplomatic
| Victory | Condition | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Grand Alliance** *(today)* | Every surviving great power is allied with you for 10 turns and you have the most systems among them. | |
| **Galactic Council** | Every 20 turns a council meets; each faction you have an embassy with votes for the empire it likes most, weighted by population. Win two elections in a row with two-thirds of the vote. | Gives embassies, gifts and opinion real weight. |
| **Peacemaker** | No war anywhere in the galaxy for 15 consecutive turns, and you are the most-liked empire. | |

### Other
| Victory | Condition | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Exploration** | Explore 90% of the galaxy and make contact with every faction. | Early-game race; best on huge maps. |
| **World Revolution** | Have 6 systems defect to you through subversion, and 3 minors as vassals. | Shadow-war victory. |
| **Assimilation** | Hold 30 systems you took from others (conquest or defection), keeping their improvements. | |
| **Score** *(today)* | Highest score at the turn limit. | Always available. |

## Questions for you
1. Which of these do you like, and which feel wrong for the factions?
2. Should a game let the host switch victories on and off individually, or offer presets (e.g. "Classic",
   "Peaceful", "Total War")?
3. Should a faction see the others' victory progress, or only its own (and what its spies reveal)?
